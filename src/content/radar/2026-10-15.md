## ☸️ Cloud / DevOps / Infra

- **Kubernetes v1.36 stable** — **Released spring 2026** [official], 80+ enhancements เน้น security, AI hardware support (DRA + GPU workload scheduling) ([CNCF](https://www.cncf.io/news/2026/04/22/cloud-native-now-kubernetes-v1-36-promotes-stability-compatibility-reproducibility/))
- **K8s 1.37 in alpha** — 1.37.0-alpha.3 ออกแล้ว, roadmap ชี้ Kueue v0.16, Volcano v1.14 (AI scheduling) ร่วม ([Kubernetes releases](https://kubernetes.io/releases))
- **Ecosystem growth** — Rook v1.19, KubeSphere v4.2.1 supporting AI/ML workloads expansion; Ingress NGINX retirement ในปี 2026 ⚠️ ([Versionlog](https://versionlog.com/kubernetes/))

→ **ภาพรวม:** K8s focus shifted to AI hardware + declarative scheduling; legacy tooling lifespan warns on migration planning
