- **Prettier, ESLint → Rust implementations** — OXC, SWC replace JS tooling, frontend performance-first era ⚠️ ([Medium — 7 AI-Powered K8s Tools](https://medium.com/devops-ai-decoded/7-ai-powered-kubernetes-tools-rewriting-cloud-native-ops-in-2026-e5e57c69cd73))
